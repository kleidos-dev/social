# AGENTS.md — social

Rules for any agent changing this repository. [README.md](README.md) says what it holds and how it is built.

## What is where

- `pack.json`: the one data file. Every image, its source, its PNG files and the platform rule each answers to; the repository cards; what is not made, and why; the previews.
- `src/`: the SVG sources, kept by hand. `src/shared/avatar.svg` (the mark alone), `src/youtube/`, `src/makertube/`, `src/cards/card.svg` (the one card template) and `src/cards/motifs/` (one small drawing per motif).
- `png/`, `previews/`, `exports.json` and the tables of `README.md`: generated by `tools/export.py`. Never edit them.
- `brand/`: the brand repository, a git submodule with the address `https://github.com/kleidos-dev/brand.git` (README, "Building", says how to point a clone at a local brand). The mark, the lockup, the colors, the fonts and the drawing code (`brand/tools/kleidos_brand/`) are read from it. Never edit anything under `brand/` from here: change it in the brand repository, then move the submodule.
- `tools/pack.py` (the data and the paths), `tools/source.py` (a source made ready to draw, and what is measured on it), `tools/cards.py` (the card system), `tools/previews.py`, `tools/export.py` and `tools/check.py` (the commands). `tests/`: their tests.
- `TRADEMARKS.md`: a byte copy of `brand/TRADEMARKS.md`. Its wording changes there, and only with the owner's yes.
- `.github/commit-check/` and `.github/workflows/commit-check*.yml`: this repository's copy of the commit-message gate, written by the `agents` repository's `sync_commit_check.py`; change the skill's files there, never the copy.

## Checks before you commit

```sh
.venv/bin/python tools/export.py --check   # after any change to a source, to pack.json or to the submodule, on the exporting machine
.venv/bin/python tools/check.py
.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests
.venv/bin/reuse lint
```

Export again (`.venv/bin/python tools/export.py`) instead of editing a generated file, commit the exported files with the change that caused them, and **look at every image you produced** before committing it: at full size, and a card also at the 640 x 320 and 320 x 160 a page shows it (`previews/cards.png` has both). CI (`.github/workflows/check.yml`) checks out the submodule and runs `reuse lint`, `tools/check.py`, the tests, and the exporter's static and tolerant checks.

## Rules

- **Everything is drawn from brand.** The mark is `<g class="mark">` with brand's five shapes; the lockup is the group brand's logo files draw, pasted whole and filled in again on every export; every color is a token's value; every word is set in brand's pinned Mona Sans; the boards are brand's copies of the board fronts, put on a shelf (`<rect data-boards="…" fill="none">`) by `kleidos_brand.boards`. Nothing is redrawn, recolored or retyped here, and no font file is ever added to this tree.
- **The mark is always whole.** The keyhole inside its ring, at any size. No ringless glyph, in any file, for any platform. An image of the mark alone (the avatar, the watermark) holds the mark and nothing else.
- **True colors, tokens only.** No opacity, tint, gradient, shadow or filter. A new color is a new token in brand first. The board fronts are the exception, as in brand: real things, drawn in their cases' true colors as the boards repository draws them, with their own unlock screens on their panels.
- **One card system.** Every repository's card is `src/cards/card.svg` with its row and its motif. Do not give a repository a layout of its own; change the template and every card changes with it. A motif keeps the motifs' line language (README, "Repository cards") and stands on the shelf; the one motif of real things, `shelf`, holds the nine board fronts at one scale on two rows, the upper one on a shelf drawn as the card's.
- **Platform rules are read, not remembered.** A rule in `pack.json` carries the address it was read at and the day. Do not make automated reads of makertube.net or of YouTube's watch pages; MakerTube's limits are cited from PeerTube's source.
- **Under the limit, as PNG.** A card weighs less than 1,000,000 bytes, GitHub's limit; the exporter falls back to a 256-color palette before it fails, and never writes a JPEG.
- **Boards come through brand.** The board fronts are read from `brand/boards/` (brand's MIT copies of the `boards` repository's fronts) when an image is drawn; no board drawing, front, icon or model is copied into this tree, and no render of a device is added. Where boards appear, all nine appear, at one scale: the StickS3 is never the only one.
- **Licenses.** The artwork (the sources, the motifs, the exported images and their previews) and the pack's data are GPL-3.0-or-later, the firmware's license; the tooling (`tools/`, `tests/`, the CI, the pins, the configuration and the commit-message gate copied from the `agents` repository) is MIT, as in brand (owner, 2026-11-17). `REUSE.toml` draws the line; a new file takes the license of what it is. The GPL here carries the two additional terms of `TRADEMARKS.md`: 7(e) (no trademark rights) and 7(c) (a modified version is marked as changed and does not misrepresent its origin). No Creative Commons license, no custom terms, no "all rights reserved"; a third-party file would need a license the OSI approves, its own entry in `REUSE.toml` and its notice in `NOTICE.md`. A license tag stands alone on its line.
- **Only what is needed now.** YouTube, MakerTube and the repository cards. Another platform is added when the owner asks for it: a source, a row and an export.
- **The vendors** are written "M5Stack" and "LILYGO" (owner, 2026-11-18), as brand's `about/facts.json` lists them.
- **English** in every file. If a Spanish text is ever added, it never says "vault" or "bóveda": the container is "llavero", its content "tus contraseñas".
- **Nothing private**: no home-directory path, private address, hardware address, serial port, host name, token or key, no value or name of a private setting, and no e-mail address but mail@kleidos.dev (the contact) and jmrplens@kleidos.dev (the supplier in `REUSE.toml`). `.gitmodules` holds brand's public address and never a local path.
- **Markdown prose is never hard-wrapped**: one line per paragraph and per list item; code, tables and fenced blocks keep their own layout.
- **Commits:** signed, under the project identity set locally in this clone, imperative English subject of at most 72 columns (aim for 50), a blank second line, body wrapped at 72 columns, no AI attribution lines. Never `--no-verify`, never a force push to `main`. Install the hook once per clone: `pre-commit install --hook-type commit-msg`.
- **Nothing is published or uploaded without the owner's yes.** Published at https://github.com/kleidos-dev/social; `main` only moves forward (squashed once, on publication), by pull request. CI here checks out the submodule from its public address. Uploading an image to a profile or a repository's settings is his to do or to ask for.
