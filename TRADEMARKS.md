## How we ask you to use the name and the mark

This part is a request. It adds no condition to the license.

Welcome, and no permission is needed:

- Writing or talking about Kleidos: press, blogs, reviews, videos, talks, teaching. Use the name, the mark and the press-kit images.
- Saying what your work is: "based on Kleidos", "a fork of Kleidos", "works with Kleidos".
- Linking to the project with its mark.

We ask you:

- Not to present a modified firmware, or another product or service, as Kleidos itself. Give a fork its own name and its own mark, and say it is based on Kleidos.
- Not to suggest that the project or its author endorses, sponsors or is affiliated with you or your product.
- Not to alter the mark: keep the keyhole inside its ring, its proportions and its colors, and use one of the files provided.
- Not to print the registered-trademark sign beside the name or the mark: neither is registered.

The project holds no registered right to enforce these with. They describe the use that keeps the name meaning one thing. A use that misleads people about where a product comes from may still be unlawful under the unfair-competition or consumer-protection law of the place where it happens, whatever this page says.
